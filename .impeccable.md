## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, HR, and hiring managers at engineering/aerospace companies, quickly scanning a single-page portfolio to evaluate Killian Boularand for an embedded-software engineering apprenticeship ("alternance"). Fast, skeptical, professional read — not a leisurely browse.

### Brand Personality
Sober, technical, precise. Stated directly in the codebase (`src/app/page.tsx` header comment): *"sobre, technique, lisible. Papier + encre, sage en accent unique, règles 1px, aucune décoration. Les photos portent l'émotion."* (sober, technical, legible; paper + ink, sage as the single accent color, 1px hairline rules, no decoration — photography carries the emotional weight, not ornament.)

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial / print-inspired minimalism. Paper (`--paper` #f7f5ee) and ink (`--ink` ramp) surfaces, one sage-green accent (`--sage` #666d57) used sparingly, hairline 1px borders doing the work shadows/cards would elsewhere. Light mode only (paper/ink is the whole premise). No glassmorphism, no gradients, no card-shadow treatments, no icon-above-heading templates. Existing motion vocabulary is already restrained: 120ms fast transitions, `cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0.2,1)` standard ease, subtle 1.03x image scale on figure hover, `prefers-reduced-motion` already respected globally.

### Design Principles
1. Motion is barely-there and purely functional — feedback and reveal, never decoration or "effect." Reinforce the "aucune décoration" ethos rather than push against it.
2. CSS-only implementation — transitions, `@keyframes`, and `IntersectionObserver` for scroll triggers. No new animation dependencies (no framer-motion/GSAP); matches current stack (Next.js 16, Tailwind v4, no motion libs in package.json).
3. One sage accent only — motion should never introduce new colors or gradients; accent color stays reserved for its existing meaning (availability dot, links, buttons).
4. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` for every animation added (pattern already established in `korvo-tokens.css`).
5. French-language, print/editorial tone throughout — animation should feel like turning a well-designed page, not like a SaaS product demo.
